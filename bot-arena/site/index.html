    How to play
    
    
  
    

  
    The rules
    
  
      
    
    1. Every tick, your bot makes one move: it sends all but 1 of the mass on one of your tiles to a neighbouring tile.
      2. 
    
    2. Moving onto a tile that isn't yours starts a fight. The bigger number wins and keeps the difference.
      3. 
    
    3. Your core (the diamond) grows by 1 every tick. Your other tiles grow by 1 every  ticks.
      4. 
    
    4. Take the enemy core to win. If nobody does before the tick limit, whoever owns the most tiles wins.
      5. 
  
    

  
    You pick the arena size and the tick limit (400 to 20000) above the Fight button.
    

  
    Your bot
    
  
    Write function bot(game) and return one move with tile.moveTo(neighbour). Return nothing to wait. Variables outside bot keep their values for the whole match.
    
  
    function bot(game) {
  // Take my biggest stack and march it at the enemy core.
  const biggest = game.myTiles.sort((a, b) => b.mass - a.mass)[0];
  return biggest.moveTo(biggest.stepToward(game.enemyCore));
}
    

  
    What you can use
    
  
    
    
    
      
    game.myTilesYour tiles (also enemyTiles, neutralTiles, and tiles for all of them)
    
      
    game.myCore, game.enemyCoreThe two cores
    
      
    game.tile(x, y)The tile at x, y
    
      
    game.tick, game.maxTicks, game.sizeThe current tick, when the match ends, and the arena width
    
      
    tile.mass, tile.x, tile.yIts mass and position
    
      
    tile.mine, tile.enemy, tile.neutral, tile.wall, tile.coretrue or false
    
      
    tile.neighborsThe tiles next to it that you can move to
    
      
    tile.moveTo(other)The move to return. other must be a neighbour.
    
      
    tile.stepToward(other)The neighbour one step closer to other, walking around walls
    
      
    tile.distanceTo(other)How many steps away other is
    
      
    tile.closest(list)The tile in list that's the fewest steps away
    
    
    
  
    

  
    Limits
    
  
      
    
    • Each tick must finish within  ms or your bot forfeits.
      • 
    
    • If bot() throws, you don't move that tick and the error shows in the console.
      • 
    
    • Bots run in a sandbox with no network access. console.log prints to the console panel.
      • 
    
    • Submit to the arena shares your bot with everyone who visits. Its code is minified and scrambled, so it isn't shown on the site, but it isn't encrypted.
