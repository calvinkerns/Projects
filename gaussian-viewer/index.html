Rendering

Toggle Updating: On re-sorts every time the camera moves. Smoothest to look at, heaviest on large scenes.

Toggle Updating: Off keeps the last sort order. You can still fly around freely, but splats will look wrong from new angles until you press Manual Update to re-sort once for where you are standing now.
